# eSpace Hubs manual

## What is eSpace Hubs?

eSpace Hubs is where eSpace engineers track their yearly performance
goals and collect the evidence behind them. You add your goals (or import
them from Zoho), and the AI suggests a tracker for each one. Some
trackers fill themselves from GitLab, GitHub, Jira and your CI builds.
Others you log yourself, week by week or month by month. Your manager
approves the trackers you build, grades your goals, and reads the review
packets you send. Admins manage accounts, signup codes and the org
chart. Each role has its own hub: the Dev Hub, the Manager Hub and the
Admin Hub.
